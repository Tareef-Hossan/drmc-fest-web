FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register for an event?

Browse fests, select an event, and fill out the registration form. You'll receive a unique registration ID after successful registration.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept bKash payments. Send the registration fee to 01814333287 and enter the transaction ID in the registration form.

Can I cancel my registration?

Yes, you can cancel pending registrations from your dashboard. Confirmed registrations cannot be cancelled without organizer approval.

How do I check my registration status?

Visit the "My Registrations" page to view all your registrations and their current status.

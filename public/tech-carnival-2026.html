FAQ

Good to know.

Who can participate?

Students at four levels: Junior (Class III–VIII), Secondary (Class IX–X), Senior (Class XI–XII, including 2026 HSC examinees) and University.

What should I bring?

A valid student/institutional ID card. It is required for campus entry and for prize collection.

What is the registration fee?

Each segment has its own fee, from free up to 1,000 BDT per team. Tick a segment under Sectors to see its fee, prize money and rules. Pay via the club's bKash personal merchant number 01814333287.

Can I join multiple segments?

Yes. Select the segments you want while registering, as long as their timings don't clash.

Are judges' decisions final?

Yes. Judges' decisions are final and binding across all technical, gaming and quiz segments.
